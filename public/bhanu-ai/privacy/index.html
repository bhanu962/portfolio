Privacy Policy

How information is handled on this website and when using Bhanu AI.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy applies to the portfolio website at bhanunarra.me and the Bhanu AI programming education assistant. Bhanu AI provides educational help with Java, Python, SQL, data structures and algorithms, debugging, coding practice and interview preparation.

2. Information you submit through the contact form

If you use the portfolio contact form, the information you provide may include your name, email address, subject and message. The contact service also processes a technical IP address for request-rate limiting and abuse prevention. The form uses validation and a honeypot field to reduce spam.

Contact submissions are sent to the site owner by email using the Brevo transactional email service. Vercel may process request data while hosting and running the serverless contact endpoint. Brevo and Vercel process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

3. Website storage

The website uses browser local storage to remember a sound preference and session storage to remember whether welcome audio has played during the current browser session. These items support website functionality. The site's source code is not configured to use Google Analytics or Google AdSense. This statement does not rule out technical storage or logs used by hosting providers or other infrastructure.

4. Bhanu AI and ChatGPT

Bhanu AI is provided as a skills-based assistant within ChatGPT. The assistant does not maintain a separate Bhanu AI user account database. When you use it, your prompts and responses are handled by ChatGPT/OpenAI. OpenAI's own privacy policy and product settings govern how OpenAI processes ChatGPT data. The site owner does not control OpenAI's independent data handling.

5. Why information is used

Information submitted through the contact form is used to read and respond to your inquiry, deliver the message by email, and protect the endpoint against spam or excessive requests. Browser storage described above is used for the related sound and audio features.

6. Retention

There is no fixed retention period for contact messages. Messages may remain in the recipient's email account and in service-provider systems according to the relevant account settings and provider retention practices. You may email a request about your information; any deletion request will be reviewed in light of applicable requirements and copies held by third-party services.

7. Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website and contact form, including Vercel for hosting and Brevo for transactional email. ChatGPT use is subject to OpenAI's own policies. Information is not intentionally sold by the site owner.

8. Your choices and requests

For questions or requests relating to information submitted through this website, contact contact@bhanunarra.me. You can also manage browser storage through your browser settings. ChatGPT data controls are managed through OpenAI and ChatGPT settings.

9. Security and changes

Reasonable technical measures are used in the contact endpoint, including input validation, spam checks and request limiting. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change; the latest version will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@bhanunarra.me.
